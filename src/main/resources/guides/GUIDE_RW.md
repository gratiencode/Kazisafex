# Kazisafe — Umuyoboro w'Igikoresho

> Murakaza neza kuri ubuyobozi bw'ibanze bwa Kazisafe. Iyi nyandiko isobanura, intambwe ku intambwe, uko ukoresha ibintu byose bya porogaramu yo gucunga ubucuruzi n'imari y'ibigo bito n'ibyoroheje.

---

## 1. Ibyo Kazisafe akora

Kazisafe ni porogaramu yuzuye yo gucunga ubucuruzi ikufasha:

- Gucunga **ibicuruzwa**, **ingero** n'**ububiko (stock)**
- **Kugurisha** ku kaunta hamwe n'amafagitire
- **Kugura**, **gutanga ibicuruzwa** n'**imisaba (réquisition)**
- **Abakiriya**, **abatanga ibicuruzwa** n'**imari**
- **Umusaruro**, **ubutaka** n'**ibarura ry'ibintu**
- **Raporo** z'imari n'imiterere y'ububiko
- Umufasha **Gratien** w'ubwenge urekora inyandiko zawe akasubiza ibibazo byawe
- Gukora **nta Internet** hamwe na synchronisation ikora mu buryo bwikora

| Ikintu | Umurimo |
|---|---|
| Menyu y'ibumoso | Kwinjira byihuse muri module zose |
| Umurongo wo hejuru | Gushakisha, imiterere, ubufasha |
| Umufasha Gratien | Ikiganiro cy'ubwenge ku iburyo |

---

## 2. Gutangira no kwinjira

1. Fungura Kazisafe uva kuri desktop cyangwa muri menyu yo gutangira.
2. Ku nshuro ya mbere, porogaramu ikora imikorere n'ububiko bw'imbere bw'aho byikora.
3. Andika **token ya activation** n'**irangamuntu ry'ikigo** byatanzwe na muharabuza wawe.
4. Hitamo **akarere kawe** (urugero: Goma, Bukavu, Kinshasa...) hanyuma **ifaranga nyamukuru** (USD cyangwa CDF).
5. Kanda **Kwinjira**.

> Porogaramu ihuza n'ububiko bw'imbere bw'aho nta Internet. Ibyandikwa bisynchroniserwa na cloud rimwe na rimwe.

---

## 3. Urugero rwo hejuru (Dashboard)

Urugero rwo hejuru rwerekana incamake y'ibikorwa byawe:

- **Amafaranga y'ubucuruzi** y'uyu munsi, icyumweru n'ukwezi
- **Ubugurishije** bugufi n'ibicuruzwa byiza
- **Iburira ry'ububiko** (ibicuruzwa byarangije cyangwa munsi y'urugero)
- **Imari iboneka** ku conta na conta
- **Imyenda y'abatanga** n'**inyandiko z'abakiriya**

Kugira ngo ubone amakuru mashya, kanda buto **Fungura bya nshya** hejuru iburyo ya karita na karita.

---

## 4. Gucunga ibicuruzwa

### 4.1 Gukora igicuruzwa

1. Fungura menyu **Ibicuruzwa**.
2. Kanda buto **Igicuruzwa gishya** (+).
3. Andika:
   - **Izina ry'igicuruzwa** n'**code-barres**
   - **Icyiciro**, **irangamano**, **icyitegererezo**
   - **Ingano** n'**ibara** (bihitamo)
   - **Ishusho** y'igicuruzwa
4. Kanda **Bika**.

### 4.2 Guhindura cyangwa gusiba igicuruzwa

- Kanda iburyo ku gicuruzwa muri listo kugira ngo **uhindure** cyangwa **usibe**.
- Gusiba ntibishoboka niba igicuruzwa gifite amateka y'ububiko cyangwa ubugurishije.

### 4.3 Ibiciro n'irangamano

Muri module y'Ibicuruzwa ushobora gucunga:

- **Ibiciro** (inyobwa, ibiryo, ibikoresho by'umuriro, ...)
- **Irangamano** n'**ibyitegererezo**
- **Amatsinda** y'ibicuruzwa

### 4.4 Ingero (unités)

Buri gicuruzwa gifite **ingero** (Igice, Kg, Katoni, Litiro...).

1. Fungura igicuruzwa hanyuma tab **Ingero**.
2. Kanda **Ongeraho ingero**.
3. Andika **ibisobanuro** n'**ubunini bw'ibirimo**:
   - Urugero: 1 Katoni = 12 Ibice → ibisobanuro "Katoni", ubunini = 12
4. Bika.

> Ingero y'ibanze (ubunini = 1) ikoreshwa mu bubiko bw'ibanze. Izindi ngero zemera ubugurishije muri katoni, paketi, nibindi.

---

## 5. Abatanga ibicuruzwa

1. Fungura menyu **Abatanga ibicuruzwa**.
2. Kanda **Umutanga mushya**.
3. Andika: **izina**, **aderesi**, **telefone**, **irangamuntu**, **RCCM**, **nimero y'imisoro**.
4. Bika.

Nyuma ushobora:

- **Guhindura** amakuru
- **Kureba amateka** y'ibyatanzwe n'imyenda y'umutanga
- **Kwishyura imyenda** ku kanya uva kuri fiche y'umutanga

---

## 6. Gutanga ibicuruzwa no kugura

**Facture y'umutanga** iba igitangwa: kwakira ibicuruzwa hanyuma bikajya mu bubiko. Urashobora kuyandika mu **buryo bubiri**:

| Uburyo | Buryahe igikoreshwa |
|---|---|
| Na **Gratien** umufasha | Facture ari ifoto cyangwa PDF, nta kwandika |
| **N'ukuboko** | Kwandika ku kibaho, igikintu ku gikintu |

### 6.1 Kwandika facture y'umutanga uhereye kuri Gratien

1. Fungura ikiganiro cya **Gratien** (umurongo w'iburyo).
2. Kanda ikinimiro cya **nyandiko** (pièce jointe) maze uhitemo **ifoto cyangwa PDF** ya facture y'umutanga.
3. Andika amabwiriza, urugero: *« Andika iki gitangwa »* cyangwa *« Injiza iyi facture mu bubiko »*.
4. Gratien asoma facture maze akora ukwayo:
   - **ibicuruzwa** (asanga ibihari cyangwa akora ibishya)
   - **umutanga**
   - **igitangwa** gifite nimero y'igice n'indangantago ya facture
   - **imisaba** (ubunini, igiciro cyo kugura, lot, itariki yo kurangira)
5. Reba amakuru yatanzwe hanyuma **emeza** mbere yo gukomeza.
6. Ububiko buriyongera: reba kwinjira mu **Kugura** n'impapuro z'ububiko.

> Gratien abona igiciro cyo kugura n'igiciro cyo kugurisha uhereye ku mafaranga ya facture. Niba ifaranga rya facture ritandukanye n'irya mbere, asaba kohereza byemejwe mbere yo guhindura.

### 6.2 Kwandika facture y'umutanga n'ukuboko

1. Fungura menyu **Gutangiza / Kugura**.
2. Hitamo **Achat**.
3. Kanda **Ongeraho**: ifomu y'igitangwa ifunguka.
4. Hitamo cyangwa ukore **umutanga** wa facture.
5. Andika **nimero y'igice** (nimero iri kuri facture) n'**indangantago**.
6. Kuri buri gikintu cya facture, ongeraho: igicuruzwa, **lot**, **ubunini**, **ingero**, **igiciro cyo kugura**, **itariki yo kurangira** n'**ibura ry'ububiko**.
7. Kanda **Bika igitangwa**.
8. Hanyuma ukore **imisaba** kugira ngo ibicuruzwa biboneke ku gurisha (reba igice cya 7).

> Igiciro cyo kugura n'igiciro cyo kugurisha bishobora kuzuzwa na Gratien ukwayo uhereye kuri facture (ifoto cyangwa PDF) nubwo wandika igitangwa n'ukuboko.

### 6.3 Lot

Buri gicuruzwa cyakiriwe gifite **nimero ya lot** ikoreshwa mu:

- Kubona itariki yo kurangira
- Gukoresha uburyo bwa FIFO / LIFO / FEFO mu gusohora ububiko
- Gukurikirana aho buri kintu gituruka

---

## 7. Imisaba (kwinjiza ububiko)

**Imisaba** ikora ububiko bushobora kugurishwa, ku gicuruzwa, ku lot no ku ngero.

### 7.1 **Achat**

1. Muri **Gutangiza**, hitamo **igitangwa**.
2. Kanda icone **+** kongeraho ikintu.
3. Imisaba yuzuza: igicuruzwa, **igiciro cyo kugura**, ubunini, lot.
4. Reba **igiciro cyo kugurisha** n'**ingero y'ubugurishije**.
5. Kanda **Bika**: ububiko bwongerwa.

### 7.2 **Depot**

Kugira ngo wakire ibicuruzwa byatanzwe uhereye ku bundi bumagazini:

1. Muri **Gutangiza**, hitamo **Depot**.
2. Kanda **Ongeraho**.
3. Listo **Indangantago y'ubumagazini** yerekana destockage byatanzwe ku karere kawe.
4. Hitamo **indangantago**: igicuruzwa, **lot**, **igiciro cyo kugura** n'**ubunini** byuzuza ukwayo.
5. Hindura niba bikenewe hanyuma **Bika**.

> Inyandiko za « Depot » ntizisaba nimero y'igice cy'umutanga: ziturutse ku bumagazini bwawe ubwako.

---

## 8. Destone n'ubumagazini

Module **Destockage** isohora ibicuruzwa mu bubiko bw'ubumagazini kugira ngo ibyohereze mu bundi bumagazini cyangwa mu karere.

1. Fungura menyu **Destockage**.
2. Hitamo **igicuruzwa**: lot ziboneka n'**igiciro cyo kugura** byerekanwa ukwayo.
3. Hitamo **ingero** n'**ubunini**.
4. Hitamo **aho bija (akarere)** muri listo.
5. Andika **indangantago** (urugero: DST12345K) n'**igitekerezo**.
6. Kanda **Ongeraho ku rutonde**, hanyuma **Emeza** kwandika destockage.

> Destockage ikuraho ububiko ku bumagazini bw'inkomoko. Ubumagazini bweza bushobora kubwakira binyuze mu misaba ya **Depot**.

---

## 9. Ubugurishije na caisse (POS)

### 9.1 Gukora ubucuruzi

1. Fungura menyu **Ubugurishije / Caisse**.
2. Shakisha igicuruzwa n'**code-barres**, izina cyangwa irangamano.
3. Kanda ku gicuruzwa kugira ngo ukiongeze mu karito.
4. Hindura **ubunini** urebe **igiciro** n'**ingero** yo kugurisha.
5. Hitamo **umukiriya** (cyangwa "Umukiriya uhishe").
6. Kanda **Kwishyura**:
   - Hitamo **uburyo bwo kwishyura** (Amafaranga, Mobile Money, Karito, Inguzanyo)
   - Andika **amafaranga yakiriwe**
   - Porogaramu ibara **amafaranga y'isubizwa**
7. Emeza: ticket icapwa (bihitamo) n'ububiko bugabanuka.

### 9.2 Ubugurishije ku nguzanyo

- Hitamo umukiriya wanditswe hanyuma uburyo bwo kwishyura **Inguzanyo**.
- Inyandiko iongerwa ku fiche y'umukiriya kandi ishobora **gusubizwa** uva muri menyu y'Abakiriya cyangwa Imari.

### 9.3 Amateka y'ubugurishije

- Tab **Ubugurishije** yerekana transactions zose.
- Ushobora **kureba ibisobanuro**, **gucapa** resiti cyangwa **guhagarika** ubucuruzi (ku ruhusa).

---

## 10. Abakiriya

1. Fungura menyu **Abakiriya**.
2. Kanda **Umukiriya mushya** wandike **izina**, **aderesi**, **telefone**.
3. Bika.

Buri fiche y'umukiriya irimo:

- **Amateka yo kugura**
- **Inyandiko** (ubugurishije ku nguzanyo bukiriho)
- **Imyishyurire** yakozwe

---

## 11. Imari

### 11.1 Conta z'imari

1. Fungura menyu **Imari**.
2. Kora conta zawe: **Caisse**, **Banki**, **Mobile Money**...
3. Andika **ifaranga** rya buri conta.

### 11.2 Ibikorwa

- **Kwinjiza amafaranga**: kongera conta
- **Gusohora amafaranga**: kuva cyangwa kwishyura
- **Ibyangijwe**: kwandika inguzanyo n'**imvo**, **amafaranga** n'**itariki**
- **Kwimurira**: kohereza amafaranga hagati ya conta

> Ibyangijwe bishobora kwandikwa ukwayo cyangwa bisomwe ukwayo kuva muri resiti na Gratien.

### 11.3 Kwishyura

- **Kwishyura umyenda w'umutanga**: hitamo igitangwa wandike amafaranga
- **Gusubiza inyandiko y'umukiriya**: hitamo ubugurishije ku nguzanyo wandike kwishyura

---

## 12. Umusaruro

Module **Umusaruro** iguha ubushobozi bwo gukora ibicuruzwa byangombwa uhereye ku bibikoresho.

1. Fungura menyu **Umusaruro**.
2. Kora **imibare** (urutonde rw'ibikoresho n'ubunini bukenewe).
3. Tangiza **umusaruro**: porogaramu igabanya ibikoresho ikongere ibicuruzwa byangombwa.
4. Kurikirana **igiciro cy'umusaruro** muri raporo.

---

## 13. Ubutaka bw'ibirimo

1. Fungura menyu **Ubutaka bw'ibirimo**.
2. Andika umutungo wawe: **izina**, **itariki yo kugura**, **agaciro**, **igihe cyo gucepa**.
3. Porogaramu ibara **gucika** ukwayo.

---

## 14. Ibarura n'ibarura ry'ibintu

1. Fungura menyu **Ibarura**.
2. Kora **ibarura** ku karere cyangwa ubumagazini.
3. Andika **ubunini bw'umubiri** bw'ibicuruzwa byose.
4. Gereranya n'ububiko bw'ibitekerezo: **itandukaniro** ryerekanwa.
5. Emeza ibarura: ububiko **buhindurwa**.

> Guhindura byera byikora inyandiko z'ububiko (kuinjira cyangwa gusohoka) n'indangantago y'ibarura.

---

## 15. Raporo

Menyu **Raporo** itanga:

| Raporo | Ibirimo |
|---|---|
| Imiterere y'ububiko | Ububiko ku gicuruzwa, lot, ingero n'agaciro |
| Ubugurishije | Amafaranga y'ubucuruzi mu gihe, ku gicuruzwa, ku mucuruzi |
| Kugura | Ibitangwa n'ibyangijwe byo kugura |
| Imari | Imikorere n'urugero rwa conta |
| Umusaruro | Inyungu, inguzanyo n'ibisubizo |
| Bilan | Ibintu, imyenda n'umutungo |
| Imari ihambaye | Amafaranga yinjira n'ayasohoka |

Kuri buri raporo:

1. Hitamo **igihe** (kuva / kugeza) n'**akarere**.
2. Kanda **Gukora**.
3. **Ohereza** muri Excel, PDF cyangwa ucapa inyandiko.

---

## 16. Imiterere

Menyu **Imiterere** (icone y'umurongo) iguhaye:

- **Ikigo**: izina, aderesi, telefone, irangamuntu, logo
- **Ifaranga nyamukuru** n'**igipimo cyo guhanahana** USD/CDF
- **Akarere** n'ubumagazini
- **Ururimi** rwa porogaramu (Igifaransa, Icyongereza, Igiswahili, Lingala, Kinyarwanda, Icyarabu, Hindi)
- **Imbaraga** eka / ijoro
- **Uburyo bw'ububiko**: FIFO, LIFO cyangwa FEFO
- **Imashini icapa** n'imiterere ya ticket
- **Abakoresha n'uburenganzira**: nde ashobora kugurisha, guhagarika, guhindura, gusiba

> Imihindagurikire yose ikorwa ako kanya. Bimwe mu byifuzo bisaba kongera gutangiza porogaramu.

---

## 17. Umufasha Gratien

**Gratien** ni umufasha w'ubwenge uherereye muri Kazisafe. Akufasha:

### 17.1 Kwandika inyandiko

Ohereza Gratien **ifoto** cyangwa **PDF** ya facture y'umutanga, resiti cyangwa ticket:

1. Kanda icone ya **inyandiko** mu kiganiro.
2. Hitamo inyandiko yawe.
3. Andika igisabwa cyawe, urugero: *« Andika iki gitangwa »* cyangwa *« Kora igitangwa kuva muri iyi resiti »*.
4. Gratien asoma inyandiko, akora **ibicuruzwa**, **umutanga**, **igitangwa** n'**imisaba** ukwayo.
5. Asaba kwemeza n'ibisobanuro mbere yo kwemeza.

> Kubandika facture y'umutanga n'ukuboko (nta Gratien), kurikira inzira yo mu **gice cya 6.2**. Kubijyanye n'imbonano (dépenses), reba **igice cya 11.2**.

### 17.2 Gusubiza ibibazo byawe

Baza ibibazo byawe mu rurimi rusanzwe:

- *« Nigute nkora igicuruzwa? »*
- *« Ububiko bwanjye bwa Coca 1.5L ni bangahe? »*
- *« Uyu mukiriya anyicizwa angahe? »*
- *« Kora bilan y'ukwezi gushize »*

Gratien asubiza akoresheje **umuyoboro w'igikoresho** cya Kazisafe n'amakuru yawe ariho ubu.

### 17.3 Amabwiriza yihariye

- `/kanuni <igisabwa>`: bika igisabwa cyawe Gratien azakurikira buri gihe.
- *« Hagarika »*: ahagarika igikorwa kirimo kugenda hamwe no kwemeza.

---

## 18. Synchronisation n'izindi zawe

Kazisafe ikora ku zawe nyinshi zisangira amakuru:

- Buri poste ikora ku **bubiko bw'imbere** (gukora nta Internet byemejwe).
- Amakuru **asynchroniserwa** na cloud ukwayo iyo Internet iboneka.
- **Synchronisation yo mu gihe** ikoresha WebSockets kuri poste zihuza.
- Ushobora guhatira synchronisation uva muri **menyu yo kuvugurura** (buto ya synchronisation).

> Mbere yo gukora, reba icone ya synchronisation hejuru: cyatsi = vugururwa, orange = itegereje.

---

## 19. Backup n'imirimo

- **Ububiko bw'imbere** bukorwa ukwayo mu myikorere ya Kazisafe.
- Kora **backup** rimwe na rimwe za dossier `Kazisafe/Media` n'ifishi y'ububiko.
- Ujye ubika **kopi ya token yawe ya activation** ahantu hizewe.

---

## 20. Gukemura ibibazo

| Ikibazo | Igisubizo |
|---|---|
| Porogaramu irabaha | Reba Internet na synchronisation itegereje |
| Ububiko butameze | Kora ibarura uemeze itandukaniro |
| Synchronisation yahagaze | Tangiza porogaramu ubundi uhatire synchronisation |
| Umubango wibagiwe | Temagana umuyobozi wawe cyangwa muharabuza |
| Imashini icapa ntibika | Reba port USB n'imiterere ya ticket muri parametre |

---

## 21. Ubufasha

Ku bufasha ubwo aribwo bwose:

- Fungura menyu **Ubufasha?** munsi y'umurongo: **umuyoboro wa PDF** w'iyi nyandiko ufunguka.
- Baza **Gratien** ku kanya mu kiganiro.
- Temagana **muharabuza** wawe cyangwa itsinda rya Kazisafe hamwe na nimero ya lisansi.

> Murakoze gukoresha Kazisafe. Uyu muyoboro uboneka kandi muri PDF uva muri menyu **Ubufasha?**.
